We propose FIRE, an extensible framework for comprehensive data quality assessment across multiple dimensions. FIRE aligns many quality signals into a unified space and integrates the characteristics of diverse data quality raters, producing a comprehensive quality signal for every data point. Experiments show FIRE outperforms other data selection methods, reaching target performance with less than 37.5% of the training data required by the random baseline.

**Keywords:** LLM · multi-dimensional data quality assessment · multi-rater integration · data strategy
